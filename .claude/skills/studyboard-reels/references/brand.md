# Studyboard brand for video

## Colours

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| navy | `#0E2A56` | Hook and end-card backgrounds, buttons |
| blue | `#1F6FEB` / `#3988FE` | Accents, eyebrows, the URL pill |
| highlighter yellow | `#FFE45C` | The one key phrase per beat (highlighter swipe or yellow text on navy) |
| paper | `#F2F5FA` with a faint blue 54 px grid | Feature-scene backgrounds |
| ink | `#0F1F3D` | Text on paper |
| muted | `#5A6A86` | Secondary text |
| course colours | leaf `#1E9E74`, tomato `#E4513B`, plum `#7A4FD6`, blue `#1F6FEB`, amber `#B86A00` | Task cards and chips, as on the real Board |

Use yellow sparingly: one highlighted phrase per beat. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.

## Type

- **Lexend** ExtraBold (800) for headlines, 600 for UI text. Headlines 96–120 px on a 1080-wide frame.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** for body text (400/700).
- Fonts live at `{{ROOT}}/website/assets/fonts/` and the template already loads them.

## Art

- App icon: `{{ROOT}}/icon-512.png`. Real app screenshots, if wanted: `{{ROOT}}/today-screenshot.png` and anything the capture tools in `tools/` produce.
- Companions (free ones, drawn by the app itself): **Hoot** the owl (`{{owl}}`), **Mochi** the sprout (`{{mochi}}`), **Inky** (`{{inky}}`), **Packy** (`{{packy}}`). They're the brand's personality: a companion reacting (hopping, a speech bubble) is a cheap, charming pattern interrupt.
- Rebuild app UI in HTML (cards, plan rows, flashcards) rather than using blurry screenshots: it stays sharp at 1080 px and can animate.

## Voice

Calm, plain, a little warm, on the student's side. Short sentences. No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changer", "AI-powered" as a headline). Light humour about student life is fine; mocking students isn't.

Lines already in use that can be reused: "Your whole semester on one board." · "Type it like you'd say it." · "Always knows what's next." · "A study buddy that quizzes you." · "Free · works in any browser · no account needed".

## What Studyboard may claim

Check the repo `README.md` (features) and `PRO-PLANS-GUIDE.md` (Free vs Pro) before stating anything specific. In particular:
- **Free:** the Board, Today's Plan, Quick Add, Exam Prep Planner, Exam Mode, Crunch Forecast, assignment breakdown, flashcards and quizzes, Match game, Focus timer and companion, school-site sync (Canvas, Brightspace, Blackboard), imports (Anki, Quizlet, Notion, Todoist), works offline, no account needed.
- **Pro** ($4.99/month, $15.99/semester, $39.99/year; two free weeks for new accounts): Studyboard AI, Record a Lecture, the PDF reader and editing, Autopilot, Smart Reminders, Quiz Me, Writing Studio, Concept Maps, two-way calendar sync and more. If a reel features a Pro feature, don't call it free; "try it free for two weeks" is accurate.
- AI is optional in Studyboard. Don't make "AI" the headline of a reel about a feature that works without it.
- Don't invent numbers (users, grades improved, hours saved) or testimonials. Hypothetical student situations are fine and expected.
- Don't use other companies' logos or trademarked app UI. Naming the school sites it syncs with in text is fine.
