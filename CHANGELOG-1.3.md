# Studyboard 1.3 (iPhone and iPad): what's new since 1.2

## For the App Store ("What's New in This Version")

Paste this into App Store Connect > your app > iOS > 1.3 > What's New in This Version (4,000 characters at most; this is about 1,100):

```
Studyboard 1.3 is a big one:

• Studyboard AI is here, with a 2-week free trial for new accounts.
• Writing Studio and Concept Maps (Beta): plan, draft and cite papers, and map how ideas connect.
• Lecture Replay: play a recorded lecture from any line of its transcript, your notes or slides.
• Exam Day Forecast: see how ready you'll likely be on exam day, with one tip to get there.

• Much faster with big terms: the app starts up to 9× faster and grading cards is far quicker.
• Better accessibility: screen readers, keyboard, larger text and high contrast work across the app.

Thank you for studying with Studyboard!
```

---

## New

- **Studyboard AI.** Studyboard's own AI for flashcards, quizzes, study notes and more, with a usage card that shows what's left. A weekly allowance resets each week. A one-time popup introduces it.
- **Two-week free trial** of Pro for new accounts (was one week).
- **Writing Studio (Pro, Beta).** Plan, draft, challenge and cite a paper, with references in sentence case automatically, and "Add to Paper" from the PDF reader.
- **Concept Maps (Pro, Beta).** Map how a course's ideas connect, with a quiz mode; a card on each course page.
- **Lecture Replay.** Play a lecture from any line of its transcript, your typed notes or slides; replay notes as they were written; Hear It.
- **Exam Day Forecast (Pro).** In each exam's Prep Plan: "Today 43% → Exam day ~72%", one line on why, one tip.
- **Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.** Today (small, medium, large, and extra large on iPad) and Up Next on the Lock Screen. Do This Next follows your plan through the day without opening the app, and changes made on other devices arrive in the background.
- **Haptics.** You feel checking off a task (a success when it clears today's plan), picking up and dropping cards and notes, crossing columns, Schedule swipes, flashcard grades (a success for the last card of a session), quiz answers (right, wrong, and a success at 80%+), Match, starting and ending a Focus session, quick add, deletes and Undo, recording a lecture, each page of Scan to PDF, stickers landing, streak days and new companion props. Settings > Haptic Feedback turns them off, and Try It checks they work.
- **New companion props are celebrated.** The prop appears in the middle of the screen in a burst of light, with Put It On and Later.
- **Companion clothing patterns (Pro):** stripes, polka dots, plaid, stars, checks and zigzag; any colour (Pro) or the palette colours (free).
- **Every companion prop can be earned** with focus hours, flashcards or practice quizzes, plus study-plan sessions and study groups; Round Glasses and Champion Medal are free to wear once earned.
- **Ask the companion about grades.** Every mark and course standing is searchable; finding one opens the course's Grades tab and highlights it.
- **Spanish.** The main screens are translated, and Spanish can be picked in Settings. Right-to-left languages mirror the layout.
- **Groups work offline.** Messages, reactions, pins, RSVPs, check-ins and group tasks are saved on the device and sent when you're back online.
- **Lectures tab:** a Back to Top button once you've scrolled well down the class list.
- **Exam and term offers** for the free plan, in the board's Notices.
- **Instagram-ready exports** (sharp 2160×2700 JPEGs).
- **Files open inside the app** on phones, in a viewer, instead of a new browser window.
- **App Store review prompt**, at a good moment and never after a paywall.
- **Schedule has a Month view** (Week / Month on a computer; Options > Month View on a phone): every class and event in the month at a glance, each with an icon for its kind (class, lab, discussion, office hours, exam, study group, work, appointment), with the day you pick listed below and See Week.
- **Schedule comes first** in Calendar, before Due Dates and Crunch.
- **Events can run past midnight** (10 PM to 1 AM); events can be deleted from their view, with Undo.
- **Take Today Off** warns what's due today and keeps it, with Undo.
- **Task Board:** tasks with no date get their own list with Add a Date.
- **Edit Task keeps a draft** across a reload and offers to restore it.
- **The add bar understands more:** "at 9" is a time, "gym every mon wed 7-8am" goes to Availability, "email/text/call…" is a 15-minute task.
- **Move Date:** Tomorrow, Next Class or a date you pick, with Undo.
- **Term dates** are guessed from all your courses together, and can be set for all courses at once.
- **After the term ends,** a "Looks like your term ended" card offers Wrap Up This Term instead of old work.

## Better on iPhone and iPad

- **Notes:** opening a note and Add Note glide up with the keyboard instead of jumping; opening Stickers no longer stretches the board; the Done row is never cut off by the keyboard.
- **Pop-ups** open and close smoothly instead of popping.
- **Grades:** a much slimmer summary at the top (about 450pt to 290pt tall).
- **Flashcards:** a slimmer review card (213pt to 126pt tall) that keeps the card-stack icon.
- **Do This Next:** the course code is a coloured pill with Take-home beside it, and Details sits at the end of the line.
- **Task Board:** a task's details stay on one line (the drag handle moved into the title row).
- **Courses and Groups:** dragging a card shows a dashed outline where it will land.
- **Groups:** the page title is a full-width pill like the other tabs.
- **Calendar:** Due Dates shows just what's due; events live on the Schedule (and its new Month view).
- **Focus timer:** the chosen mode (Focus / Short Break / Long Break) is one clean pill.
- **Files:** Convert has an icon; a file's actions are in one ⋯ menu.
- **Recently Deleted:** all the filters fit on one line.
- **Whiteboard:** "All" instead of a cut-off "All Whitebo…"; palm rejection for Apple Pencil on iPad.
- **The date under the logo** always fits, shortening instead of being cut off.
- **iPad sideways:** pages start below the clock and battery, with a soft fade in the theme's colour.
- **The companion** moves above the keyboard and back, stands level with the Add button on iPad, and no longer wanders across the screen when Courses opens.
- **PDF reader and editor:** one compact top row, tools by your thumb, typing moves with the keyboard, and the Edit bar fits on one line.
- **Phones:** buttons don't stay highlighted after a tap, and a question with three answers stacks them full width.

## Faster

- **With a big term** (400+ tasks, 2,500 cards, 300 notes): the app starts about 9× faster, typing no longer stalls, and grading a card on a phone is about 6× quicker.
- **Animated theme scenes** draw off the main thread: the same look, much smoother (for example the ocean theme went from 14 to 53 frames a second on a slow device).

## Accessibility

- **Screen readers:** flashcard answers stay hidden until flipped, focus returns where you were after a save, and search results are announced.
- **Keyboard:** the tab order follows the screen, arrow keys work in the month grid, and Esc ends a study session.
- **Larger text:** Large and Extra Large reach all text; High Contrast works on every theme.
- **Reduce Motion:** the app follows your device's setting until you choose in Settings.

## Fixes

- **Settings that switched back by themselves** after two quick changes (such as the whiteboard's light/dark) now stay put.
- **Review intervals:** Hard, Good and Easy always differ, including near an exam (Easy is never later than 2 days before it).
- **Grades:** saving a mark finishes the task; a warning when weights pass 100%; needed scores round up.
- **Calendar:** 50-minute classes show their course, and timed in-person exams block plan time.
- **Notes:** a long formula in a sticky note shrinks to fit, and a note with missing text no longer breaks Notes.
- **Imports:** impossible imported dates are set aside to check instead of breaking the plan.
- **Calculator:** recognises typed names like sqrt, sin, log and pi.
- **School site syncs:** they say plainly when they fail, with Sign In or Try Again, and a synced task you've moved can be resynced.
- **Wording:** many small wording and layout fixes throughout.
