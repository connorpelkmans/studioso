## How to comment (so it helps rather than hurts)

- **No links, no "check out our app".** Instagram hides or limits brand comments that look like promotion, and students scroll past them. The profile does the selling: a good comment makes people tap the name.
- **Be early.** Turn on post notifications for Tier 1 and 2. The first hour is when comments get the most views and likes.
- **Add to the joke or the feeling,** in Studyboard's calm, warm voice. The companion persona works well here.
- **10–15 genuine comments a day**, spread out. Don't paste the same line twice (it reads as spam and can get the account limited).

Example comments:
- On an exam-week meme: "week 11 has no right to be this personal 😭"
- On a burnout post: "you're not behind, you're just tired. one small thing tonight is enough 💛"
- On a study-tips reel: "the 'review the night after the lecture' tip saved my midterms. pairing it with a calendar that shows the crunch weeks early is elite"
- On a Canvas or due-date meme: "the Canvas notification that says 'due in 1 hour' instead of 'due in 2 weeks' 😶"
- As Hoot: "🦉 hoot hoot, that's three exams in one week. i'm calling the professor"
