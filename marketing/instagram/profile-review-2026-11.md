# Instagram profile review (Nov 2026)

Checked with the "follows" lens (`.claude/skills/studyboard-reels/references/diagnose.md`) from a screenshot on 2026-11-15: 2 posts, 3 followers, 0 following, 51 views in 30 days.

**Diagnosis:** at this size the profile isn't what's holding growth back: almost nobody is reaching it yet. The main lever is **distribution** (more reels, posted consistently, plus engaging in the student niche). The profile fixes below are still worth doing now, so that the visitors the reels do bring turn into followers.

## Changes, in order of impact

1. **Name field: add the search keyword.** Instagram search matches the name field, not just the username. Change "Studyboard" to **Studyboard · Student Planner** (or "Studyboard | Study Planner").

2. **Bio: say who it's for, the outcome, why it's easy, and what's coming.** The current bio ("syncs to your school site and tells you what to do next!") is close, but "school site" is vague, and it doesn't say it's free or give a reason to follow. Students know their platform's name, so use it. Options (each under 150 characters):

   > Your whole semester on one board 📚
   > Syncs Canvas, Brightspace & Blackboard. Tells you what's next.
   > Free, no account 👇

   > The calm student planner 📚
   > See crunch week coming, weeks ahead
   > Free · works with Canvas & Brightspace
   > 📌 Finals survival series

   (Everything here is in the README: school-site sync is read-only, on desktop, for Brightspace, Canvas and Blackboard, with calendar links elsewhere.)

3. **Grid and pins: make it look like a series, not two one-offs.** Post the crunch week reel next and **pin it**, then post the rest of a "Finals survival" series (Exam Prep Planner "3 days out", Exam Mode, Rough Week Rescue) with the same navy hook covers. A visitor should see 3 consistent covers and think "more of this is coming". Pin the "Hi, we're Studyboard" carousel second as the explainer.

4. **Highlights: add 3.** "Start here" (what it is, in 3 stories), "Finals" (the series), "Free?" (what's free and what's Pro, answering the obvious question). Use navy covers with the yellow icon so they match.

5. **Following 0 → engage in the niche, as the brand.** Follow and leave genuinely useful comments on StudyTok and studygram creators, student associations (Instagram is already suggesting the BCIT Student Association) and campus accounts. Early on, thoughtful comments on bigger accounts send more profile visits than anything in the bio. Keep the brand voice: calm and helpful, no "check out our app".

## Smaller notes

- The bio link goes to `studyboardapp.com/website` (the marketing site), while reel end cards say `studyboardapp.com` (the app itself). Both work. The marketing site is better for cold visitors from the bio; the app is better from a reel where they've already seen it working. Keep it this way, but be aware of the difference.
- Logo profile photo: recognisable and fine as is.
- Cadence: aim for 3–4 reels a week during finals season. Use Instagram's **Trial Reels** if the account has them, to test hook variants on non-followers.
