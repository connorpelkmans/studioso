## What we've learned

- (Add one line each time the numbers show something, e.g. "POV hooks held 2x better than question hooks.")
- Pre-flight (five lenses) on the crunch week reel caught two gaps before posting: no on-screen comment prompt, and a payoff too small to notice. Both fixed.
- vidIQ outliers (Nov 2026): the biggest student-niche hits were relatable text-over-static reels, not demos, and small accounts broke out. Testing that format alongside the fix reels.
- Quality pass (Nov 15): rebuilt the relatable reels on the top outlier's measured structure (real person at frame 0, persistent native caption, cut at ~2 s, drop on a cut at ~4 s, loop), and gave every reel owned music and sound effects (they were silent).
- Relatable reels need a product payoff for a business account (spotted by Connor): added a 3.4 s fix-and-end tail to all three, keeping the native opening.
- Pacing and polish (Connor's review): no hard cuts or flashes (cross-dissolves), more reading time (17 s structure, payoff cards held 3 s or more), sentence case, and the real Studyboard wordmark on every end card.
- Branding and layout (Connor's review): logo lockup = app icon above the official wordmark; everything centred on the frame (calendars centred on the day squares); a little more energy (yellow key words, overshoot, a small confetti burst and ding at the payoff, companions on the end card).
- Polish (Connor's review): fixed flicker (a 12 Hz shake aliasing at 30 fps) and the end-card logo flash; captions now arrive with what they describe; reaction shots get 3 s; logo lockup C (app icon + Studyboard in Lexend). `check_glitches.py` now runs on every render.
- Calendar fixes now redistribute visibly (Connor's review): Week 11's load moves into Weeks 10 and 9 in sequence, conserving the total, with rings and pops as each week receives it.
- Copy pass (Connor's review): one yellow highlight per caption on the punchline, twist or benefit; exclamation marks on turns, payoffs and wins ("Way better!").
- Added an entertainment-first set (Connor: less like ads, genuinely relatable): no pitch in the video, a small sign-off, brand in the caption. Compare their shares and follows with the feature reels.
