# Content plan: features ranked by impact (Nov 2026)

How each feature is scored (1–5 each, out of 30):
- **Timely**: does it hit what students feel *right now*? Finals are 2–4 weeks out.
- **Pain**: how strong is the emotion?
- **Sendable**: would a student forward it to a friend or group chat? Sends are Instagram's strongest signal for reaching new people.
- **Visual**: can the fix be *seen* in about 2 s?
- **Free**: no paywall between the viewer and the payoff.
- **Evidence**: does the vidIQ outlier research back the theme?

**Why this order:** 1–3 hit finals-season emotions that peak over the next three weeks (grades, exams, final papers), and each has a fix you can see in two seconds. The "what do I need on the final" question is one of the most-searched student questions every December. After finals, 4–8 become the "new term" set for January (Today's Plan, school sync and Quick Add fit the start of term).

**Format for each:** the same proven structure as the same-week reels. A real student at frame 0 with a native caption, the problem shot cut on a music hit, a punchline shot, then "plot twist", 2 s of the feature fixing that exact problem, and the end card. 12 s, looped on the beat. Each uses different footage, so the feed doesn't repeat.

## Briefs for the top 3

### 1. What You Need (Grade Tracker)
- **Caption (hook):** "me doing the math on what i need on the final to not fail this class"
- **Problem shot:** a scribbled notebook equation (0.2×80 + 0.3×64 + 0.15×78 + 0.35×**?** = 75), terms slamming in on the hits, then "?" pulsing red.
- **Turn:** "plot twist: it does the math for you"
- **Fix:** a Grade Tracker card: marks so far, then **72.2% so far** counts up, then "To reach your goal of 75%, you need about **80** on the final". Caption: "studyboard tells you exactly what you need on the final".
- **Maths check:** quizzes 20% at 80, midterm 30% at 64, labs 15% at 78 = 46.9 of 65 = 72.2%. (75 − 46.9) ÷ 0.35 = 80.3.

### 2. Exam Prep Planner
- **Caption (hook):** "3 exams in 6 days and i haven't started studying for any of them"
- **Problem shot:** a week strip with three exam chips and empty days; "0 study sessions planned" stamps in.
- **Turn:** "plot twist: it plans the studying for you"
- **Fix:** add an exam plus topics (Shaky / OK / Strong), then study sessions drop across the days: Learn, Review, Practice test, Light review the day before. Caption: "studyboard spreads your studying across the days you have left".

### 3. Big assignment breakdown
- **Caption (hook):** "my 10 page paper is due friday and so far i've written my name"
- **Problem shot:** a big task card, "Final paper · due Fri", stuck at 0%, with a ticking countdown.
- **Turn:** "plot twist: it breaks it down for you"
- **Fix:** tap Break It Down, and 5 milestones schedule themselves backward from Friday into free time: pick a topic, find sources, outline, draft, proofread. Caption: "studyboard turns one scary task into small steps on your free days".
