## The research each reel is built on
| Finding | Source | How it's used |
|---|---|---|
| Testing yourself beats rereading: after 1 week, ~61% recall after repeated testing vs ~40% after repeated studying | Roediger & Karpicke (2006), *Psychological Science* | Reel 1's result, cited on screen |
| Practice testing and spacing are "high utility" techniques; rereading and highlighting are "low utility" | Dunlosky et al. (2013), *Psychological Science in the Public Interest* | Captions, and the reason for the whole set |
| Memory fades fast without review (the forgetting curve); reviews spaced over time keep it | Ebbinghaus (1885); Cepeda et al. (2006) meta-analysis | Reel 2, labelled "illustration": no invented percentages |
| Retrieving the material (e.g. filling in a map from memory) beats building a concept map with notes open | Karpicke & Blunt (2011), *Science* | Reel 4's "fill it in from memory" beat |
