Switching from Quizlet

Move Your Quizlet Sets to Studyboard, Free

Bring a Quizlet set into Studyboard in one paste.

  1. 1In Quizlet, open a set you made and choose Export. Copy the text (each term and definition is on its own line).
  2. 2Open Studyboard and choose Paste a List.
  3. 3Paste, then Add Cards. Start studying right away.

Paste My Quizlet SetFree. No account needed.

Quizlet only offers Export on sets you created. For someone else's set, copy the terms you need into a list, one per line, with a dash between the term and the definition.

Why students switch

  • Studying is free. Unlimited decks, cards, quizzes and Match. No lives to lose and no waiting between imports.
  • Spaced repetition you can see. Every card says when it's coming back and why, and cards are pulled in before your exam.
  • A hint when you're stuck. Mochi, your study companion, gives a nudge before you see the answer, and can explain it after.
  • Your whole semester in one place. Deadlines from Canvas, Brightspace or Blackboard, a plan for today, grades and study groups.
  • Private by design. No ad or tracking tools and no session recording. It works offline, with no account needed.
