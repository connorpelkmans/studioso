Privacy Policy

Last updated: DATE

Studyboard is run by YOUR NAME OR COMPANY ("we"). This page says, in plain words, what we collect and why. Questions: support@YOUR-DOMAIN.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never readable by us).
  • Your planner: the courses, tasks, notes, flashcards and settings you choose to sync, and the files you upload. They are stored so your devices stay in step. We don't read them or use them for anything else.
  • Your devices: a name and type for each device signed in to your account, and the last time it connected, so we can apply the device limit of the free plan.
  • Usage totals: how much synced data and file storage your account uses, so we can apply the storage limits of each plan.
  • Your plan: whether you have Pro, until when, and how you got it.
  • Study groups: if you join one, the display name you choose and what you post there are visible to its members.

Payments

Card payments on this website are handled by Stripe. Your card number goes straight to Stripe and never touches our servers or this website. We keep a Stripe customer id and your subscription status. In the App Store or Google Play versions, purchases are handled by Apple or Google and RevenueCat, who tell us only that a subscription is active.

Anonymous crash reports

When the app hits an error (a crash, a failed sync or a broken screen) it can send a short, anonymous report so we can fix the bug. A report holds the kind of error and where in the code it happened, the app version and build, the platform (website, installed app, desktop or iPhone/iPad app), whether you have a free or paid plan, the theme in use, and the last few actions of the app itself (such as which tab was open). It never contains your tasks, notes, courses, files, email address, account id, AI keys, file names or anything you typed, and addresses are cut down to a page name with no query or fragment. It is not linked to you: it carries only a random number made on your device (not your account) that is deleted when you turn reports off. Reports are used only to find and fix bugs, are not used for advertising or tracking, are never sold or shared, and are kept for about 30 days. They are sent to our error-monitoring service (Sentry) or to our own Supabase project, over HTTPS, with no tracking script on the page.

This is on by default, and off by default if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Turn it off any time in the app: Settings, Send Anonymous Crash Reports.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or show ads.
  • This website uses no advertising or analytics trackers (the anonymous crash reports described above are not analytics: no pages or clicks are counted) and sets no tracking cookies. It stores your sign-in and your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

Who else handles your data

Studyboard's accounts and storage run on Supabase. Optional features you switch on may send data to the service behind them, for example your own AI key to the AI provider you picked, or your school's calendar address to that school's system. We only do this when you turn the feature on.

Your choices

  • You can export your data from the app (Backup) at any time.
  • To delete your account and the data in it, write to support@YOUR-DOMAIN from the email on the account. We delete it within 30 days. Payment records may be kept as long as the law requires.
  • You can sign out of all your devices by changing your password.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can only read its own rows. Plans can only be changed by our payment system and by us, never from your device. No system is perfectly safe; if something affecting you goes wrong we will tell you.

Children

Studyboard is made for students. If you are under 13 (or your country's age), please use it with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app or by email.
