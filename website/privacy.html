Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026

Studyboard is a planner for courses, deadlines, notes and flashcards, run by Connor Pelkmans, who publishes it as Studyboard ("we"). This page explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and how to delete it. The short version: your work stays on your device unless you choose to sync it, there are no ads, and nothing in Studyboard tracks you or sells your data.

What stays on your device

Your courses, tasks, notes, flashcards, files, settings, themes, daily backups, AI keys and your school-site connections are saved on the device you use (in the browser's storage on the website, or in the app's folder on desktop and phone). If you turn on folder backups on desktop, copies are also written to the folder you pick. Nothing leaves your device unless you use one of the optional features below.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Studyboard has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it sets no tracking cookies. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising. The only things stored in your browser are the ones needed to work: your data, settings and sign-in. The one thing Studyboard can send on its own is an anonymous crash report, described next.

Anonymous crash reports

When Studyboard hits an error (a crash, a failed sync or a broken screen), it can send a short report so we can fix the bug. A report holds the kind of error and where in the code it happened, the app version, the platform (website, installed app, desktop or iPhone/iPad app), whether you have a free or paid plan, the theme in use, and the last few actions of the app itself (such as which tab was open). It never contains your tasks, notes, courses, files, email address, account id, AI keys or anything you typed, and it is not linked to you: it carries a random number made on your device that is not your account (deleted when you switch reports off) and a one-way scrambled form of your network address, scrambled with a code that changes every day so it can't be traced back or matched from one day to the next, kept with the report only to stop one device from flooding us with reports. We do not use reports for advertising or tracking and never share them. They are kept for about 30 days. This is on by default (off if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control). Turn it off any time in Settings, Send Anonymous Crash Reports. On iPhone and iPad, Apple may also give us crash logs through App Store Connect if you chose to share app analytics with developers in your device's settings. Reports go to our own Supabase project, and, if we turn it on, to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), an error-tracking service that receives the same anonymous report and keeps it for no more than 30 days.

Your account and synced data

If you create an account, we store your email address and your password, which is kept only as a salted hash by our hosting provider, Supabase (Supabase Auth). Signing in also syncs your planner between your devices, so your courses, tasks, notes, flashcards, settings and file records are stored on our Supabase project, in your account. Each account can only read its own rows. We also keep the name and type of each device signed in to your account, how much storage your account uses (for the plan limits), and your plan status. Your AI keys stay on the device unless you turn on AI Keys on All My Devices in Settings (or use Add AI to Another Device, described under AI below): then they are locked on your device with a passphrase only you know, and only the scrambled copy is stored, so we cannot read them. They are always left out of the backup files you export. Your newest companion chats are also synced to your account.

Like any online service, the people who run the server can technically reach what is stored on it. We only look at it to keep the service working, fix a problem you ask us to look into, or comply with the law.

Uploaded files

Files you attach (for example PDFs and images) are stored in a private storage area of our Supabase project, in a folder that only your account can open. Photos you choose for your own theme are stored the same way, so they follow you to your other devices. They count toward your plan's storage limit and are deleted when you delete them or your account.

Optional: AI features, with your own key

AI is off until you add your own key from Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI and agree, in a prompt that names the company, to send content to it. When you use an AI feature, the content it needs (for example task titles, notes, a syllabus or PDF you import, flashcards, your question, or the text of your items when you use search by meaning) is sent straight from your device to the provider you chose, using your key. It does not pass through our servers. If you're signed in and haven't added a key, you can try AI a few times for free: those requests go to Studyboard's trial server, run on Cloudflare, which passes them to an open AI model (Google's Gemma) hosted by Cloudflare and sends the answer back. The trial server keeps only a count of your tries, under your account id. It never stores what you send. If you use Add AI to Another Device, your keys are locked (encrypted) on your device with a one-time code, and only the locked copy is left with our key relay, which runs on Cloudflare. It is deleted as soon as your other device collects it, or after 10 minutes at most, and we can't unlock it, because the code never reaches our servers. That provider's own terms and privacy policy apply, and they decide how long they keep it. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products, so do not send anything private. You can switch AI off and remove your key at any time.

Optional: your school's learning platform

If you connect Brightspace, Canvas or Blackboard, the connection stays on your device. On desktop you sign in on your school's own page (Studyboard never sees your password) and the sign-in is kept in a private store on your computer. If you paste a calendar link instead, it is saved on your device; on the website and phone the link is passed through a small function on our server that fetches the calendar for you and stores nothing. Studyboard only reads from your school's site. What it brings in (due dates, grades, announcements) becomes part of your planner and syncs like everything else if you have an account.

Optional: reminders, calendar link, study groups

Phone and desktop reminders need your device's push address and your upcoming reminders (task titles and times for the next two weeks) to be stored on our server so a notification can arrive when Studyboard is closed. The notification itself is delivered by your browser or phone maker's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla). Calendar Sync stores a private link token and your choices so your calendar app can fetch your deadlines. Study groups, shared decks and Study Together rooms share the display name and the decks, deadlines and messages you choose to share with the people in that group. Turn any of these off, or leave a group, and it stops. Voice and shortcut capture (optional, for example "Hey Siri, add a task") stores a private capture token (only a hash of it, shown to you once) and the short notes you send, in an inbox on our server until your app collects them; you can revoke the token any time, and everything is deleted with your account.

Bug reports and feedback

If you send a report, it includes what you wrote, an optional screenshot you attach, an optional email address for a reply, and basic technical details (app version, browser and device type, screen size, your plan and recent error messages). It contains none of your tasks or notes unless you type them. When you report an AI response, the form also fills in that response, which you can shorten or remove before sending. Only the app's owner can read reports.

Payments

Pro is sold through Stripe (website and desktop) or through the App Store and Google Play (iPhone, iPad and Android apps). Stripe and the app stores handle your card and billing details; we never see or store them. We receive only your plan status: whether you have Pro, when it ends and where you bought it. If you pay with Stripe we also keep a Stripe customer id so we can open your billing page. Purchases in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps are checked through RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives your account id (a random number, not your email), the store's purchase record and your device's platform so it can confirm the subscription with Apple or Google and tell us your plan status.

Email delivery

Emails we send you (confirming your address, resetting your password, signing in) are sent through our email delivery provider, Supabase, which handles your email address and the message so it can be delivered. We send no marketing email.

Other services we use

Studyboard runs on Supabase (accounts, sync and storage). On the website, fonts, the sign-in library and the PDF reader are served from the same site as the page, so no other third party is contacted for them. If the sign-in library's copy can't be loaded the page falls back to a public content network (jsDelivr), which can then see your IP address as for any web page. Studyboard also contacts the AI provider you chose, only when you use AI, and Stripe when you pay on the website. If you download the Windows or Mac installer from our website, the file is delivered by GitHub, which can see your IP address as for any download.

How long we keep things

  • Your account and planner: until you delete them. Deleted items are remembered for 45 days only so your other devices can catch up.
  • After you delete your account: your data is removed from our database and storage straight away. Encrypted backups can hold a copy for up to 30 days before they are overwritten; nothing is restored for a deleted account.
  • Payment records: Stripe and the app stores keep them as tax and accounting law requires. We keep only anonymous plan and payment-event records with no link to your account.
  • Crash reports: kept for about 30 days, then deleted. They are anonymous and not linked to your account.
  • Bug reports: kept only as long as needed to fix the problem, and never longer than 180 days (screenshots attached to a report are deleted after 30 days). Reports sent while signed in are deleted with your account.
  • Free trial record: if you started a free trial, we keep a one-way scrambled code (a hash) made from your email address after your account is deleted, only so the same address can't start another trial. It can't be turned back into your email address.

Your choices and your data

  • Export: Settings, Data and Backups, Export a Backup File gives you everything in one file.
  • Get a copy of your data (access and portability): Settings, Data and Backups, Export My Data makes a ZIP with one JSON file and spreadsheet (CSV) files of your tasks, notes, grades, flashcards, courses, schedule and study sessions. It is made on your device, works offline, and never includes AI keys, sign-in details or device ids.
  • Deleted by mistake: things you delete wait in Settings, Data and Backups, Recently Deleted for 30 days (synced with your account like the rest of your data), then are removed for good.
  • Delete everything on this device: Settings, Data and Backups, Clear All Data. Clearing your browser's site data also removes it.
  • Delete your account and online data: Settings, Account and Sync, Delete My Account and Data. This removes your account, synced data, uploaded files, reminders, devices, calendar links and plan record from our server, and cancels a card subscription for you. Study groups you own pass to another member, or are deleted if nobody else is in them, and your group messages are deleted. Subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play must be cancelled by you in your store account, because we cannot cancel them. You can also email us from the address on the account to ask for deletion.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to its use. Use the steps above, or contact us.

International transfers

Our servers are in the United States (US West). Supabase, Stripe, RevenueCat, Sentry, our email provider and the AI provider you choose may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where the law requires it we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Children

Studyboard is for students aged 13 and over. In the European Economic Area and the UK, where the age of digital consent is higher in some countries (up to 16), a person under that age needs a parent or guardian's permission to make an account. If you are younger than 13, please do not create an account. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can only read its own rows, and plans can only be changed by our payment system, never from your device. No system is perfectly safe; if something affecting you goes wrong we will tell you.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the effective date above and tell you in the app. Questions or requests about your data: support@studyboardapp.com.
