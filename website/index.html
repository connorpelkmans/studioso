What's inside

Everything a term throws at you, sorted.

Built for how students actually work: lots of classes, shifting due dates and too many PDFs. The whole planner is free.

Board

Drag work from To Do to In Work to Done. Every class has its own color, and every task knows how much of your grade it is worth.

Timeline & calendar

See the weeks ahead as bars, or day by day. Add your shifts or work and Studyboard knows when you are really free.

Today's Plan

Picks what to work on next, puts deadlines first, fits it into your free time and tells you when a day is overbooked.

Rough Week Rescue

When a week is overloaded, Studyboard suggests what to push and what cannot move, and applies it in one step.

Quick add

Type bio quiz fri 2pm worth 5% and the course, date, time and weight are filled in. Snap a photo or add by voice on your phone.

Focus & Study Together

A focus timer with your study companion, and shared countdown rooms so your group can focus at the same time.

Flashcards & quizzes

Spaced-repetition flashcards and quizzes. Turn on AI to make a deck from your own notes or a PDF.

Grade Tracker

Add your marks, see your current and projected grade and your pass mark, and find out what you need on the rest.

Notes & stickers

Sticky notes you can pin, decorate with stickers and keep beside the course they belong to.

Search Everywhere

Press Ctrl+K or ⌘K to search tasks, courses, cards, notes, events and the text inside your files. Ask With AI finds things by meaning.

Syllabus & school sync

Drop in a syllabus, or connect Brightspace, Canvas or Blackboard once and your assignments, quizzes and exams land on your board.

Study groups

Share decks, quizzes and deadlines, plan sessions with RSVPs, take on a weekly focus challenge and chat. Free groups hold 3 people; Pro holds 100.

Sync & backups

Start on your laptop, finish on your phone.

Sign in once and your board follows you. Changes sync between your devices, and anything you do offline catches up the next time you are connected.

  • Sync on your devicesTasks, notes, decks and files follow you. Free syncs on 2 devices; Pro has no limit.
  • Works offlineKeep working on a bus or a plane. Everything catches up the next time you are online.
  • BackupsA copy of your planner stays on your device. Pro adds 30 days of online backup history.
  • Your calendar app tooAdd a live calendar link so deadlines, classes and events show up in Apple, Google or Outlook Calendar.
  • Your data is yoursNo ads, no tracking. Delete your account and everything stored online from Settings, any time.

Questions

Good to know

Is there a web version?

Yes. Open Studyboard in any modern browser and it works right away. Use your browser's install option, or Add to Home Screen on a phone, to open it like an app.

When can I download the apps?

The Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone and Android apps are being prepared. The download buttons above switch on as soon as each one is published, and your account and data work the same everywhere.

Is the planner really free?

Yes. The whole planner, flashcards, quizzes, Grade Tracker, Today's Plan, Focus and Search Everywhere are free, with no ads and no time limit. Pro adds unlimited devices, more storage, bigger groups and every theme.

Do I need an account?

You need one Studyboard account to sync between devices and to buy Pro. Create it in the app or on the account page.

How does the AI work?

AI is optional and off until you turn it on. You add your own free Gemini key (or a Claude or ChatGPT key), and the content for a request goes straight from your device to the company you chose.

What happens when my trial ends?

If you don't cancel, Pro continues at the price you chose. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

What if I cancel?

You keep Pro until the end of the time you paid for. Nothing you made is deleted; free accounts just have the free limits.

How do I manage billing?

Sign in on the account page and choose Manage billing to change your card, see invoices or cancel.
