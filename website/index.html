Companion & AI

Meet your study companion.

Every theme has three little companions who live in your scene, study with you and cheer on your wins. Enable AI and your companion becomes your helper for the whole app.

  • Ask About This. Your companion explains the card, note, task, file or course on your screen.
  • Exam Prep Planner. Turns an exam date, your notes and your decks into a day-by-day plan.
  • Make study material. Flashcards, quizzes and study sets from your notes or a PDF.
  • Syllabus in, deadlines out. Drop in a syllabus, and get quick time estimates for many tasks at once.
  • Ask With AI. Search finds things by meaning and tells you where it found them.
  • Grows with you. Companions unlock accessories as you study and celebrate when you finish things.

AI is optional and off until you add your own key (a free Google Gemini key, or Claude or ChatGPT). Each question sends only the parts of your planner it needs, straight from your device to the company you chose.

Your companion, Mochi, ready to answer questions about your own deadlines, courses and notes. Free comes with 6 companions, Pro adds 204 more.

Sync & Backups

Start on your laptop, finish on your phone.

Sign in once and your board follows you. Changes sync between your devices, and anything you do offline catches up the next time you are connected.

  • Sync on Your DevicesTasks, notes, decks and files follow you. Free syncs on 2 devices; Pro has no limit.
  • Google Drive and OneDriveKeep files and lecture recordings in your own Drive or OneDrive, free. They show up in their courses and open on any device.
  • Works OfflineKeep working on a bus or a plane. Everything catches up the next time you are online.
  • BackupsA copy of your planner stays on your device, and it can back up every hour to your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Pro adds 30 days of online backup history.
  • Your Calendar App TooAdd a live calendar link so deadlines, classes and events show up in Apple, Google or Outlook Calendar.
  • Your Data Is YoursNo ads, no tracking. Delete your account and everything stored online from Settings, any time.

Download

Get Studyboard on every device.

Use it in your browser today, get the Windows app from the Microsoft Store, install the Mac app, or add the School Sync extension to Chrome. The iPhone and Android apps are on their way, and everything syncs between them.

Windows: get Studyboard from the Microsoft Store, which installs it safely and keeps it updated. The Mac installers are not code-signed yet, so your computer may warn you. They are safe to open if you downloaded them from this page.

How to open them

Windows: if you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac: open the .dmg and drag Studyboard to Applications. The first time, if macOS says it can’t verify the app, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Studyboard. Older macOS versions let you right-click the app and choose Open instead.

Questions

Good to know

Is there a web version?

Yes. Open Studyboard in any modern browser and it works right away. Use your browser's install option, or Add to Home Screen on a phone, to open it like an app.

When can I download the apps?

The Windows app is on the Microsoft Store and the Mac app is available now, and the Linux, iPhone and Android apps are being prepared. Those buttons switch on as soon as each one is published, and your account and data work the same everywhere.

Is the planner really free?

Yes. The whole planner, flashcards with smart scheduling, quizzes, Match, Grade Tracker, Today's Plan, Focus, Search Everywhere, study groups of 5 and Google Drive and OneDrive are free, with no ads and no time limit. Pro adds unlimited devices, more storage, bigger groups, lecture recording, two-way calendar sync and every theme.

Can I find flashcards for my course?

Yes. The Flashcards and Quizzes pages hold sets shared by students, plus sample sets from @studyboard made from public course materials. Search by course code or school, then study online or add a set to Studyboard in one tap.

Do I need an account?

You need one Studyboard account to sync between devices and to buy Pro. Create it in the app or on the account page.

How does the AI work?

AI is optional and off until you turn it on. You add your own free Gemini key (or a Claude or ChatGPT key), and the content for a request goes straight from your device to the company you chose. Your companion is the face of those features.

What happens when my free week of Pro ends?

You're simply on the free plan again, with nothing to cancel and nothing charged. Everything you made stays. Pro themes go back to a plain theme the next time you open Studyboard. During the free week, storage, devices and group size stay at the free amounts, so nothing is over a limit when it ends.

What if I cancel?

You keep Pro until the end of the time you paid for. Nothing you made is deleted; free accounts just have the free limits.

How do I manage billing?

Sign in on the account page and choose Manage billing to change your card, see invoices or cancel.
