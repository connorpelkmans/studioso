### Wisteria Walk (id: wisteria) — Nature and Places
- Scene: Standing inside a long wisteria tunnel: gnarled, twisting vine trunks hold up a roof of fresh pinnate leaves hung with lavender and white racemes, a mossy flagstone path runs away under the arches, and through the far opening a little red arched footbridge crosses a still pond in front of a soft tree line and flowering azaleas.
- Palette hint: day sky #B9D6EE → #EAEEF6 → #F6EEF0, blossoms #C8B4EC / #A486D8 / #7A5AB8 and white #FBF8F2, leaves #4C7444 → #9CC66A; night sky #0E0A24 → #2E2260 → #45367A, foliage #1A1838 → #3A4470, blossoms glowing #C8BEF2 / #E8E4FA
- Loop: The racemes sway on their stalks as breezes roll down the tunnel arch by arch, sun flecks shimmer and drift on the stone path, soft light shafts slant in from the far opening, single petals flutter down (and ring the pond when they land on it) and the water glints. At night it is lit only by the moon: the blossoms glow pale against the deep violet foliage, moonlight pools on the path, the moon shimmers in the pond and stars twinkle in the opening.
- Finish moment: A warm breeze rushes down the tunnel: the racemes swing one arch after another, a cascade of lavender and white petals streams away toward the bridge, the light shafts blaze, and the petals settle on the pond in rings of ripples. At night the blossoms flare brighter and the petals glow as they drift under the moon.
- Companions:

  2. Meji, White-eye Chick — "A tiny green songbird chick with white spectacles and a big appetite for nectar." idle: headTilt, wingFlutter, topBob; cheer: hop
  3. Kero, Pond Froglet — "A little green froglet who naps on a lily pad beneath the arched bridge." idle: headTilt, wave, topBob; cheer: bounce

### Bluebell Wood (id: bluebells) — Nature and Places
- Scene: An English beech wood in spring: smooth silver-grey trunks with flared roots stand at every depth, their crowns spreading in layered tiers of fresh lime-green leaves, and a carpet of bluebells rolls away between them into a violet haze, with a winding leaf-litter path, nodding bluebells and white wood anemones in the foreground.
- Palette hint: day sky #CFE4EE → #EEF5D8 → #F8F6E0, canopy #7CB246 → #B8DE68, trunks #AEB4B4 / #8E9698, carpet #7078D2 → #A6A2E2 haze; night (blue hour) sky #0A1230 → #22346C → #33488A, carpet #28336A, bells #6A70DA
- Loop: The foreground bluebells nod and sway as breezes pass, sunbeams slant down through the canopy and slowly drift, sun flecks shift over the carpet and the path, motes float in the light and a fresh beech leaf twirls down now and then. At blue hour mist drifts low over the bluebells, the bells glimmer softly and stars show between the crowns.
- Finish moment: A breath of wind rolls through the wood: a wave of nodding runs through the bluebells, a shimmer of violet light rolls away across the carpet into the distance, sunbeams fan down through the canopy and fresh beech leaves spin down. At night the shimmer glows through the mist.
