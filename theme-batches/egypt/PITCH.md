### Egypt (id: egypt) — Around the World
- Scene: The three pyramids of Giza (stepped stone courses, the smooth casing remnant at the top of Khafre, Menkaure with its three queens' pyramids) and the Sphinx rising over warm golden desert, with the green ribbon of the Nile in front: a palm grove on the far bank, date palms, papyrus reeds, a white-sailed felucca on the water, a mud-brick village edge with a whitewashed dovecote, water jars and a rowboat drawn up on the bank.
- Palette hint: day sky #9CCBEA → #F4E6C6 → #F6C98A; night sky #0C1232 → #1E2050 → #4E3260
- Loop: The felucca's sail fills and it drifts along the Nile (a smaller one far off the other way), palm fronds sway, papyrus nods, heat shimmers over the sand, a camel caravan plods across the far dunes, ibises wade and feed in the shallows, pigeons circle the dovecote. At night a huge starry sky with the Milky Way over the pyramids (and now and then a shooting star), the river reflects the moon, the felucca carries a lantern and village windows glow.
- Finish moment: The sun (the moon at night) slides down until it lines up exactly on the tip of the Great Pyramid and its capstone flashes gold, light sweeps down the stone faces of all three pyramids, and a flock of ibises rises off the Nile across the glow.
