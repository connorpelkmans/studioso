## Batch n9

### Firefly Bayou (id: bayou) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A still, dark-green bayou of bald cypress standing in the water, with their knees, curtains of Spanish moss, water lilies and duckweed, a warm low sun glowing through the haze. At night a big moon hangs over mirror-black water.
- Palette hint: day sky #A9C9B6 → #EADFAE → #F6CC8C; night sky #07141A → #123230 → #1C4038
- Loop: Everything is mirrored in the still water. Mist drifts low over it, the Spanish moss sways, drops from the moss ring the water, ripples lap round the cypress knees and the sun or moon lays a glittering path. By day golden motes float in hazy sun rays. At night hundreds of soft golden fireflies glow and blink over the water, each with its own reflection.
- Finish moment: The fireflies flash together in waves that roll across the water, the closed lily buds open one after another with a glow, and a ribbon of light spirals up from the water to ring the moon (by day, the low sun), then drifts apart.
