## Batch n7

### Slot Canyon (id: canyon) — Nature and Places
- Scene: Looking up and along a narrow, winding sandstone slot canyon whose walls flow in stripes of rose, rust, orange and gold, with a crack of sky above and a sandy floor dotted with pebbles, grass tufts and a little flowering shrub.
- Palette hint: day sky #86C0EA → #F4F2E0 → #F8BE7C, walls #6A2C4A → #CC6A41 with lit edges #FFB56C; night sky #070A22 → #1E1C4A → #45305E, walls #1A1532 → #3D2A5C with moonlit edges #8E9EE0
- Loop: Shafts of sunlight fall from the crack of sky and slowly breathe as clouds pass, dust motes glow where the light catches them, cottonwood fluff drifts down through the beams, and every so often a trickle of sand pours from a ledge onto a little pile. At night a strip of stars and the Milky Way fills the gap, stars twinkle, moonlight silvers the wall edges, mica glints on the walls and the odd shooting star crosses the crack.
- Finish moment: The sun lines up with the slot: one great beam pours straight down to the floor, a wave of glow runs down the walls from top to bottom, glittering dust swirls up through the light, and a ring of pale desert primroses opens where the beam lands (a silver moonbeam and white evening primroses at night).
- Companions:
  1. Dusty, Horned Lizard Baby — "A tiny horned lizard with a crown of soft spikes. Basks first, then works." idle: headTilt, tailSwish, wave; cheer: hop
  2. Pinto, Pincushion Cactus — "A tiny cactus with a flower crown. Prickly outside, soft-hearted inside." idle: topBob, wave, bounce; cheer: hop
  3. Kit, Kit Fox Pup — "Ears as big as its dreams. Hears a deadline coming from three dunes away." idle: earTwitch, tailSwish, wave; cheer: hop

### Mirror Flats (id: saltflats) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A huge white salt flat under a thin sheet of water that mirrors the sky perfectly: big pastel sunset clouds and a low sun above, their twins below, far-off low mountains on the horizon, a row of small salt mounds in the shallows, and islands of hexagonal salt crust where it's dry.
- Palette hint: day sky #97AEDC → #C2B6E2 → #F2C2D0 → #FFE2C2 (mirrored a touch deeper below the horizon); night sky #080B26 → #141A44 → #2E2D66 → #57487E
- Loop: Small clouds drift across the sky and across their reflections, breeze streaks slide over the mirror and little rings spread on it, and the low sun glitters along its path on the water. At night the stars twinkle together with their reflections, the Milky Way lies in the water as well as the sky, and shooting stars streak across both skies at once.
- Finish moment: Rings of ripples run out across the flat, the sun throws a pillar of light through itself and its twin, every cloud above and below blushes gold and sparkles burst where the sun meets the horizon. At night a meteor shower falls through the sky while its mirror image falls up through the water, and the moon glows brighter.
- Companions:
  1. Flora, Andean Flamingo Chick — "A fluffy chick who will be bright pink one day. Already blushes a lot." idle: wingFlutter, headTilt, topBob; cheer: wingFlutter
  2. Mira, Mirror Droplet — "A drop of the mirror lake. Reflects the sky, and your best side." idle: bounce, sway, topBob; cheer: hop
  3. Vicu, Vicuña Calf — "A wobbly calf in the softest wool there is. Loves a long, calm view." idle: earTwitch, headTilt, tailSwish; cheer: hop
