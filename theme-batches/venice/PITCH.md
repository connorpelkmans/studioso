### Venice (id: venice) — Around the World
- Scene: Down a Venetian canal from a high window: faded ochre, rose and terracotta palazzos rise from green-teal water on both banks, with Gothic ogee windows, stone balconies, string courses, worn plaster showing brick, water doors with steps and striped mooring poles, funnel chimneys and wooden altanas. A humpbacked brick-and-stone footbridge with lanterns crosses the canal, washing hangs on a line above it, two gondolas lie moored by the near steps, and at the canal's end the lagoon opens on St. Mark's domes, the Doge's palace and the brick campanile.

- Loop: A gondolier rows away down the canal, under the bridge and out towards the lagoon; ripples break up the reflections, the moored gondolas bob, geraniums and the washing sway, shutters swing in the breeze and pigeons peck, hop and flutter off the bridge. At night windows and the bridge lanterns glow and their light shimmers on the water.
- Finish moment: The campanile's bells ring out in rings of sound, a flock of pigeons bursts up over the domes, a fleet of gondolas glides out across the lagoon and a sweep of golden light runs down the water towards us.
