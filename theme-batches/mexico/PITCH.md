### Mexico (id: mexico) — Around the World
- Scene: A colonial hillside town in the style of Guanajuato and San Miguel de Allende: flat-roofed houses in saffron, cobalt, coral, lime and magenta stacked up two dry hills with blue jacarandas between them, a pink sandstone cathedral with a tiered Gothic spire, side towers and a crowd of pinnacles, a tiled saffron-and-cobalt dome behind it, painted colonial facades with iron balconies and arcades around a cobblestone plaza, box-trimmed laurels, a wrought-iron bandstand, a talavera fountain, potted agave and cacti, iron lamps and a balloon cart, dry mountains behind. Papel picado strings cross the top, a jacaranda leans in at the top left and bougainvillea spills off a pergola at the top right.
- Palette hint: day sky #8CCBF0 → #E4F2F6 → #FCE2C6; night sky #0F1636 → #2A2458 → #6A3458
- Loop: Jacaranda petals drift down, the papel picado flags swing and turn on their strings, the fountain splashes from its upper bowl into the basin, two hummingbirds hover and dart between the bougainvillea sprays, the balcony geraniums sway, the jacarandas stir, and the balloon seller's bunch bobs on its pole. At night the cathedral is floodlit pink and gold, windows twinkle up the hills, the bandstand's bulbs and the iron lamps glow, and stars and a crescent moon come out.
- Finish moment: The cathedral bells swing and ring out in golden rings of sound, the bandstand lights chase, a gust sweeps confetti and papel picado ribbons across the plaza, and the balloon seller's balloons float up one by one over the spires (the cart fills up again afterwards).
