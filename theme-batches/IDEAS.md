## Batch n1

### Hot Spring (id: onsen) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A steaming outdoor bath in snowy mountains, ringed by stones, pines and a little wooden bathhouse.
- Palette hint: day sky #BFD8EE → #E8F1F8 → #F6EEE6; night sky #1C2340 → #3A3F6B → #6B5A7A
- Loop: Steam curls off the water and bends in the breeze, snowflakes land on the surface and vanish in tiny rings, a bamboo water spout fills, tips and clacks. At night the stone lanterns flicker and steam glows in their light.
- Finish moment: A capybara drifts in with a yuzu balanced on its head, sighs happily, and the yuzu rolls off and bobs away across the pool.
- Companions:
  1. Kapi, Onsen Capybara — "Will sit in warm water for as long as you study. Possibly longer." idle: topBob, earTwitch, headTilt; cheer: bounce
  2. Momo, Snow Monkey Baby — "Keeps a snowball on its head at all times, for balance." idle: earTwitch, wave, tailSwish; cheer: hop
  3. Yuzu, Floating Yuzu — "A sunny citrus that bobs, rolls and never quite sinks." idle: topBob, sway, sparkle; cheer: spin
