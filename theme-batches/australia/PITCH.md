### Sydney Harbour (id: australia) — Around the World
- Scene: Sydney Harbour from the botanic garden shore: the Opera House's white tiled shell sails (two overlapping groups and the little restaurant shells, chevron tile texture, bronze glass walls) on its sandstone podium, the steel arch of the Harbour Bridge with its granite pylons behind, the city skyline and its tower beyond, the north shore with red-roofed houses, deep blue water with ferries and sailboats, and in front a gum tree with a sleeping koala, bottlebrush and waratah flowers, and a sandstone sea wall.
- Palette hint: day sky #86C8F2 → #D8EEF8 → #F8EAD0; night sky #0A1434 → #1A2A5C → #4A3A6E
- Loop: Green-and-cream ferries cross leaving wakes, sailboats tack, water glints, gum leaves sway, the dozing koala's ear twitches, cockatoos and rainbow lorikeets fly in to perch on the gum and the bottlebrush, a pelican glides low. At night the sails glow softly, the bridge is lit, the skyline sparkles and reflections ripple.
- Finish moment: Fireworks burst over the bridge in gold, red and blue, the Opera House sails light up in colours, and a flock of rainbow lorikeets swoops across.
