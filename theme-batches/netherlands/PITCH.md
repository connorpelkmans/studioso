### Netherlands (id: netherlands) — Around the World
- Scene: A flat Dutch polder under a big sky. A straight canal runs to the horizon between a row of tall gabled canal houses (step, bell, neck and spout gables) and tulip fields in bold stripes of red, yellow, pink, orange and purple, with three windmills with lattice sails along the water, a white double drawbridge on the dike road, a weeping willow, bicycles leaning on a railing and a flower cart of potted tulips on the quay.
- Palette hint: day sky #A9CDEE → #E4EFF8 → #F3F0DA; night sky #101A38 → #23305A → #4A4A72
- Loop: The windmill sails turn at different speeds, light ripples across the tulip stripes while the near tulips sway in waves, cumulus clouds sail across the big sky, ducks paddle in the canal, a bicycle rides along the dike road (and waits at the bridge), and the drawbridge rises whenever the houseboat comes up or down the canal. At night the canal houses' windows and the street lamps glow and shimmer in the canal, and stars come out over the fields.
- Finish moment: All the windmills spin up together, the tulip stripes bloom one row after another like a wave of colour (petals fly up from the near rows), and a V of geese flies across the sky.
