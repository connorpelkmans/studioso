### Glowing Bay (id: glowbay) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A sheltered tropical cove between two dark rock headlands topped with jungle, a crescent of white sand, leaning palms and gentle waves, with distant islands on the horizon.
- Palette hint: day sky #48A0CE → #E4D8B2 → #F6BE8E, sea #52C4C2 → #1590A8; night sky #02061A → #0C2250 → #183A68, sea #0B2747 → #04132A
- Loop: Swells roll into the cove and wash up the sand, palm fronds sway, the low sun's glitter path twinkles, spray bursts off the headland rocks and a tiny sailboat crosses the horizon. At night the breaking crests and the shoreline glow electric blue with bioluminescence (drawn as glowing water, not creatures), each wash leaves a fading glowing line on the wet sand, plankton sparks wink in the dark cove, spray off the rocks glows, and the Milky Way arches overhead with shooting stars.
- Finish moment: One big wave rolls across the whole bay with a blazing electric-blue crest (golden sparkling foam by day), bursts into glowing spray against both headlands, surges far up the beach and leaves the whole shoreline glittering as it drains away.
