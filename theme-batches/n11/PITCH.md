## Batch n11

### Poppy Meadow (id: meadow) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A rolling alpine meadow full of red poppies, cornflowers, daisies and tall grasses, with a winding stream, a lone oak on a gentle rise and blue snow-capped mountains beyond, under big summer cumulus clouds.
- Palette hint: day sky #6FB0E6 → #C8E4F6 → #EEF3E2; night sky #0A1330 → #26356A → #3C4A7A
- Loop: Gusts roll through the tall grass and flowers along the bottom edge, soft cloud shadows slide across the meadow, glints run down the stream and a poppy petal drifts off now and then; two tiny birds cross the far sky once in a while. At night the meadow is moonlit and silvered, the poppies and daisies close into buds, mist drifts low over the hills, stars twinkle and a shooting star passes.
- Finish moment: A warm gust sweeps across the meadow: every flower nods in turn, a ribbon of poppy, daisy and cornflower petals spirals up into the sky, new poppies pop up across the meadow behind it and sunbeams fan out from the sun. At night the gust opens the sleeping flowers, which glow softly under a brightening moon.
- Companions:
  1. Scarlet, Red Poppy — "A sunny red poppy who turns to face you the way she faces the sun." idle: headTilt, sway, wave; cheer: spin
  2. Skip, Brook Pebble — "A smooth stream pebble with a quartz stripe and a tuft of moss on top." idle: topBob, headTilt, sparkle; cheer: bounce
  3. Billow, Summer Cumulus — "A big puffy summer cloud who makes shade whenever you need a rest." idle: sway, sparkle, bounce; cheer: spin

### Glowing Bay (id: glowbay) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A sheltered tropical cove between two dark rock headlands topped with jungle, a crescent of white sand, leaning palms and gentle waves, with distant islands on the horizon.
- Palette hint: day sky #48A0CE → #E4D8B2 → #F6BE8E, sea #52C4C2 → #1590A8; night sky #02061A → #0C2250 → #183A68, sea #0B2747 → #04132A
- Loop: Swells roll into the cove and wash up the sand, palm fronds sway, the low sun's glitter path twinkles, spray bursts off the headland rocks and a tiny sailboat crosses the horizon. At night the breaking crests and the shoreline glow electric blue with bioluminescence (drawn as glowing water, not creatures), each wash leaves a fading glowing line on the wet sand, plankton sparks wink in the dark cove, spray off the rocks glows, and the Milky Way arches overhead with shooting stars.
- Finish moment: One big wave rolls across the whole bay with a blazing electric-blue crest (golden sparkling foam by day), bursts into glowing spray against both headlands, surges far up the beach and leaves the whole shoreline glittering as it drains away.
- Companions:
  1. Kai, Sprouting Coconut — "Washed up on the beach, put down roots, and decided to stay." idle: topBob, headTilt, wave; cheer: bounce
  2. Lumi, Glow Droplet — "A drop of glowing seawater that shines brighter with every splash." idle: sway, sparkle, topBob; cheer: spin
  3. Marina, Sea Glass — "A bit of glass the sea polished for years into something soft and lovely." idle: headTilt, sparkle, wave; cheer: hop
