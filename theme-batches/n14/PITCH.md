## Batch n14

### Lotus Lake (id: lotus) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A wide, still lake at dawn crowded with giant round lotus leaves standing on their stalks at many different heights, pink blooms, buds and seed pods among them, patchy lotus beds fading into mist, weeping willows on the far shore and soft lavender hills behind the low sun.
- Palette hint: day sky #86AFDA → #E9CFCF → #FADDBE, water #F6DCC6 → #93B6CC; night sky #070D26 → #1D2958 → #2E3C6C, water #2A3864 → #091128
- Loop: The near lotus plants sway in the dawn air, dew drips off a leaf now and then and rings spread on the water, loose petals drift and rock on the surface, a petal lets go of a bloom and spins down, mist ribbons slide along the far shore and the sun's path shimmers. At night the leaves are moonlit, the buds are closed, the water is mirror dark with a moon path, stars twinkle and a shooting star passes.
- Finish moment: A wave of warm light spreads out over the lake from the sun, the near buds unfurl one after another into full blooms that breathe out golden pollen sparkles, rings ripple across the water, petals lift on the breeze and sunbeams fan through the mist. At night the buds open and glow softly pink under the moon.
- Companions:
  1. Nelu, Lotus Blossom (nature object) — "A pink lotus who opens a little wider every time you learn something." idle: headTilt, sway, wave; cheer: spin
  2. Dabble, Mandarin Duckling — "A fluffy little duckling who paddles between the lotus leaves at dawn." idle: headTilt, wingFlutter, topBob; cheer: wingFlutter
  3. Hopsy, Lotus Leaf Froglet — "A tiny froglet who naps on lotus leaves and hops up to cheer you on." idle: headTilt, wave, bounce; cheer: hop

### Sea Stacks (id: seastacks) — Nature and Places
- Scene: Golden limestone sea stacks and an arch rising from a turquoise sea below a long wall of tall coastal cliffs that recedes into the haze (Twelve Apostles style), a little beach in a bay, surf foaming round every foot, the low late-afternoon sun with gold-edged clouds, and a green clifftop with heath, a limestone outcrop and wildflowers in front.
- Palette hint: day sky #5896D2 → #E6DCBC → #F6C68E, sea #3C92BA → #3DC0B8, rock #B87A48 / lit #ECB86C; night sky #040A1E → #15284E → #22365E, sea #132648 → #07142A
- Loop: Swells roll in across the sea and pass behind the stacks, the surf swells and bursts into spray round each stack's foot, a ripple of foam runs along the foot of the cliffs, the wash slides up the little beach, the glitter path twinkles and the clifftop grass, sea thrift, daisies, yarrow and sea lavender sway. At night: a crescent moon and faint Milky Way over the dark silhouettes, silver surf and shooting stars.
- Finish moment: One great swell rolls in from the horizon with a blazing crest (gold by day, silver by night), bursts into tall spray against each stack in turn, flares white along the cliffs and up the beach, the stacks' sunlit faces glow and sunbeams fan out from the low sun.
- Companions:
  1. Stacy, Sea Stack (nature object) — "A golden limestone stack who has stood tall through ten thousand tides." idle: topBob, sway, wave; cheer: bounce
  2. Waddles, Little Penguin Chick — "A fluffy little penguin chick who waddles up from the beach at sunset." idle: waddle, headTilt, sparkle; cheer: wingFlutter
  3. Mocha, Fur Seal Pup — "A velvety fur seal pup who suns herself on the warm rocks below the cliffs." idle: earTwitch, finWiggle, headTilt; cheer: bounce
