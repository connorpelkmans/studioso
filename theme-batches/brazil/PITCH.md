### Rio de Janeiro (id: brazil) — Around the World
- Scene: Rio from the beach: Corcovado's granite peak with Christ the Redeemer (arms outstretched) on its summit, a favela of bright little houses (coral, yellow, turquoise, blue, lilac, orange, white) packed densely up the green hill below it (painted, brick and concrete, with water tanks, antennas, dishes, laundry, alleys and tangled power lines), downtown towers (balconied apartment blocks, glass offices, Art Deco and modernist slabs) along the shore, Sugarloaf's granite dome with the cable car on its wires from Morro da Urca, sailboats on the bay, and a wide golden beach with surf, striped umbrellas, beach towels, starfish, a coconut kiosk, royal and coconut palms and the Brazilian flag on a tall pole, with rainforest leaves (banana, monstera, heliconia) framing the near corner and a toco toucan on a branch.
- Palette hint: day sky #7CC6F0 → #D4EEF8 → #FCE8C4; night sky #0C1838 → #1E2C5E → #6A3A5E
- Loop: Crests roll in and the foam slides up the sand and back, the two cable cars glide between Urca and Sugarloaf, palm fronds and jungle leaves sway, the toucan tilts its head, blinks and hops along its branch, frigatebirds glide sideways across the sky, the Brazilian flag ripples on its pole, sailboats drift with their reflections, the umbrellas flutter, clouds drift. At night Christ the Redeemer glows on Corcovado, the favela windows twinkle, the kiosk's string of bulbs lights up, the far shore's lights shimmer in the water, the moon lays its path on the bay and the cable cars glide by lit up.
- Finish moment: A sunset blaze behind Sugarloaf (the sky turns gold and rose, the sun sinks behind the dome, its west face glows and a golden path runs over the bay), a flock of scarlet and blue-and-yellow macaws and green parrots bursts out of the forest across the bay, and samba-coloured confetti is tossed up over the promenade and drifts down.
