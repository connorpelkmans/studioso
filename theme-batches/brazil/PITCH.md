### Rio de Janeiro (id: brazil) — Around the World
- Scene: Guanabara Bay in Rio: Sugarloaf's granite dome rising from the water, streaked and green at its foot, with the cable car on its wires from the forested Morro da Urca, green granite hills with bare rock faces and the city's apartment blocks along the far shore, sailboats on the bay, a long curving golden beach with surf, the black-and-white wave mosaic of the promenade with lamps, royal and coconut palms, striped beach umbrellas and a coconut kiosk, and rainforest leaves (banana, monstera, heliconia) framing the near corner with a toco toucan on a branch.
- Palette hint: day sky #7CC6F0 → #D4EEF8 → #FCE8C4; night sky #0C1838 → #1E2C5E → #6A3A5E
- Loop: Crests roll in and the foam slides up the sand and back, the two cable cars glide between Urca and Sugarloaf, palm fronds and jungle leaves sway, the toucan tilts its head, blinks and hops along its branch, frigatebirds circle high over the bay, sailboats drift with their reflections, the umbrellas flutter, clouds drift. At night the promenade lamps curve along the beach, city and hill windows twinkle, the far shore's lights shimmer in the water, the moon lays its path on the bay and the cable cars glide by lit up.
- Finish moment: A sunset blaze behind Sugarloaf (the sky turns gold and rose, the sun sinks behind the dome, its west face glows and a golden path runs over the bay), a flock of scarlet and blue-and-yellow macaws and green parrots bursts out of the forest across the bay, and samba-coloured confetti is tossed up over the promenade and drifts down.
