## Batch n16

### Saguaro Sunset (id: saguaro) — Nature and Places
- Scene: The Sonoran desert at sunset: a great many-armed saguaro crowned with white blossoms in front, more saguaros with long shadows across the floor and tiny ones out to the horizon, ocotillo fans tipped with red flowers, prickly pear with magenta fruit, silvery brittlebush full of yellow daisies, red boulders and a banded red butte, three jagged purple ranges rimmed with light, and the sun sinking behind them under long pink and gold cloud streaks.
- Palette hint: day sky #4E3C8C → #D4588A → #F87C60 → #FFAA58 → #FFD88E, floor #D08068; night sky #04061A → #1E1E52 → #4A3266, floor #2C2644
- Loop: The sun glows and breathes, ocotillo canes and brittlebush daisies sway in the evening breeze, dry grass ripples along the bottom and golden dust motes drift through the low light. At night the moon rises slowly from behind the ranges, saguaros stand in silhouette with moonlit rims, stars twinkle under a faint Milky Way and shooting stars cross the sky.
- Finish moment: The sky blushes and the sun fans out its rays while every saguaro bursts into bloom, crown after crown across the desert from left to right, each puff of white blossoms sending up gold pollen. At night the blossoms open glowing under the moon (saguaros really bloom at night) during a meteor shower.

  3. Pokey, Tortoise Hatchling — "A desert tortoise hatchling. Never rushes, never quits, always arrives." idle: headTilt, tailSwish, bounce; cheer: hop

### Baobab Dusk (id: baobab) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A red dirt road running straight to the horizon between pairs of giant Grandidier's baobabs (smooth columns with flat crowns of stubby branches and leaf tufts), their inner edges rimmed with afterglow; shallow puddles on the road mirror the pink sky and the trees, flooded paddies shine in the grassland, distant baobabs dot the horizon, and long pink cloud streaks glow apricot from below.
- Palette hint: day sky #6E66A8 → #A87AAE → #EE94A2 → #FFB890 → #FFD8A2, road #B2604A, grass #8E7A5A; night sky #02041A → #0A1238 → #1E2456 → #4E3A66
- Loop: The puddles waver as the breeze ruffles them (their mirrored sky and trees shimmer) and breeze streaks slide across them, the grass along the bottom ripples, the afterglow at the end of the avenue breathes and warm dust motes drift. At night a broad Milky Way with a dark dust lane rises from the end of the avenue, stars twinkle in the sky and in the puddles, and shooting stars streak across both.
- Finish moment: The sun peeks out once more at the end of the avenue and fans its rays up between the trunks; a beam of light runs down the road towards you, each pair of baobabs flashes gold along its trunk as it passes, each puddle turns to a golden mirror with a spray of sparkles, and the sky blushes. At night the light is silver, the Milky Way brightens and a meteor shower falls, mirrored in the puddles.

  3. Loko, Panther Chameleon — "A baby panther chameleon. Changes color with every mood, mostly happy ones." idle: headTilt, tailSwish, sparkle; cheer: bounce
