## Batch n13

### Maple Ravine (id: maples) — Nature and Places
- Scene: A Japanese mountain ravine in autumn. Two maples lean in from the banks so their crimson, orange and gold canopies arch over the view. A clear stream drops from a little waterfall at the head of the gorge and steps down through wide pools, each one spilling over a low rock lip between mossy boulders. Natural stone steps climb the right bank past a stone lantern. Forested slopes of red, orange, gold and dark cedar rise behind, with hazy blue ridges and mist beyond. Ferns and fallen leaves fill the foreground.
- Palette hint: day sky #86B6DE → #B4D2E8 → #F4DCBE, canopy #BE2626 / #DE4A2A / #F08632 / #F6BE44, water #4E8C8A; night sky #070B22 → #222C58 → #353A66, canopy #3E0E18 → #7A3A2C, water #25314E with silver light
- Loop: Maple leaves drift down and tumble. Some land on the pools and ride the falls from pool to pool. Water streams over every lip, foam churns at the foot of each fall, rings spread into the pools, glints wander over the water, mist drifts low over the ravine floor and the hanging sprays sway. At night the full moon glows behind the red maple silhouettes, the water turns silver, the stone lantern burns softly, stars twinkle and a shooting star passes now and then.
- Finish moment: A gust shakes the canopy. A whirl of crimson, orange and gold leaves blows off the trees and spirals up the ravine. Sunbeams (moonbeams at night) fan down through the gap in the trees, the pools glitter, the stone lantern flares and a raft of leaves rides down every fall.
