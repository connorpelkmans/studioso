## Batch n10

### Blue Ice Cave (id: icecave) — Nature and Places
- Scene: Inside a glacier ice cave: scalloped walls in bands of translucent turquoise and blue, brightest at the mouth, with icicles along every ring of the ceiling and a frozen meltwater floor between low banks of ice. Through the mouth at the far end: a snowy peak, a slope of small pines and a bright sky.
- Palette hint: day sky #4F9CDA → #B8DDF5 → #F6FAFD, ice bands #E2FAFE → #62C8E6 → #1A5396; night sky #070B26 → #1A2A5C → #3A4C80, ice bands #5A7ED0 → #19224E
- Loop: Light glows through the thin ice and drifts slowly, drips swell on the icicle tips and fall into the meltwater with little rings, snow falls past the mouth and the odd flake blows in toward you, shafts of light and motes hang over the floor, the ice glints. At night an aurora dances outside the mouth and tints the ice green and pink from within.
- Finish moment: The light floods in: a wave of glow runs out through every band of ice, the icicles chime with light one after another, and diamond dust spirals in through the mouth. By day a prism of colour lands on the floor; at night the aurora flares and the wave washes the walls in green and pink.
