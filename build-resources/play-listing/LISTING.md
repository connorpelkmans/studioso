## Full description (max 4000)
Studyboard is a calm planner for students. It turns your courses, deadlines and exams into a plan for today, then helps you study for them.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Your data stays on your phone. An optional free account syncs it between your devices. No ads and no analytics.

TODAY'S PLAN
• Every task and deadline in one place, ranked into a plan that fits the hours you have
• Add a task by typing a sentence, like "bio quiz fri 2pm worth 5%"
• Share text or a photo from any app to add it as a task
• Work, shifts and other commitments are kept free, so the plan only uses the time you really have

PLAN AHEAD
• A timeline and a schedule for classes, exams and events
• The Crunch Forecast colors every week of the term, so a week with three exams and a paper shows up weeks ahead
• Exam Prep Planner: enter the exam date and topics, and it spreads review sessions across the days before
• Big assignments split into milestones scheduled backward from the due date

COURSES AND GRADES
• Import a syllabus PDF, track grades and see what you need on the next assignment
• Set a target grade for each course

STUDY
• Flashcards, quizzes and a match game
• Notes with stickers, files, and search across everything
• A focus timer with sounds, and a study companion that cheers you on

REMINDERS AND QUICK ADD
• Deadline reminders on your phone
• A Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts to add a task or capture a photo in seconds

SCHOOL SITES
Connect Canvas, Brightspace, Blackboard, Moodle, Schoology or Google Classroom (read only) to bring in your assignments and due dates.

YOURS TO KEEP
• Undo on every delete, and a Recently Deleted bin for 30 days
• Import from Anki, Quizlet, Todoist and Notion
• Export all your data any time
• Delete your account and data from Settings

OPTIONAL AI HELPERS
Use your own Gemini, Claude or ChatGPT key. Each question sends only the parts of your planner it needs.

STUDYBOARD PRO (OPTIONAL)
Free to use. Pro adds extras such as Insights, smart flashcards, lecture recording, PDF reading with highlights, two-way calendar sync, Autopilot rescheduling and version history. Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed to your Google Play account. It renews unless you cancel before the period ends. Manage or cancel it in Google Play > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions.
